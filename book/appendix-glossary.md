# Glossary {.unnumbered}

Every headword taught in the book's Words to Learn tables, in Samoan alphabetical order. Diacritics are folded and interfiled: there is no separate glottal-stop or long-vowel section. An unmarked spelling is listed before a marked one on a tie. "Ch" is the chapter where the word is first taught.

## Suffixes

|---|---|---|

| *=a* | the ending that makes a describing verb of a noun: being affected by what the noun names | 50 |
| *=ga* | the ending that makes a noun of a verb: something that comes of the event, or the event itself as a whole | 50 |
| *=gatā* | the ending that builds a difficult-word out of the verb it goes on; it is never a word standing on its own | 40 |
| *=gōfie* | the ending that builds an easy-word the same way | 40 |
| *=ia* | a long-form ending some speakers use, usually with a consonant in front of it | 32 |
| *=ina* | a long-form ending: the same job, preferred in formal speech and writing | 32 |
| *=na* | a long-form ending, uncommon: recognise it, and build with *=a* or *=ina* | 32 |
