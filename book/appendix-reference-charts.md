# Reference Charts {.unnumbered}

Quick-reference tables for the pronoun, possessive, article, tense-aspect-mood-particle and demonstrative systems this book teaches, reproduced from the chapters that print them.

## Pronouns

The preverbal pronoun set, taught whole in Chapter 2 as two grids: one person, then more than one.

| Samoan | English |
|---|---|
| *ʻou* | I |
| *ʻe* | you (one person) |
| *ia* | he, she |

| Samoan | English |
|---|---|
| *tā* | we two (you and I) |
| *mā* | we two (but not you) |
| *tātou* | we (you and I, and others) |
| *mātou* | we (others and I, but not you) |
| *ʻoulua* | you two |
| *ʻoutou* | you all |
| *lā* | they two |
| *lātou* | they |

Taught in Chapter 2. The independent forms *aʻu*, *ʻoe* are a separate, bounded set and are not part of this grid.

## Possessives: my things, by number and specificity

The four forms of "my", crossing one thing against more than one, and a particular thing against any thing, taught in Chapter 25.

| The thing possessed | *a*-word form | *o*-word form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| one particular thing | *laʻu* | *loʻu* | my |
| one thing, any one | *saʻu* | *soʻu* | a ... of mine |
| more than one, particular | *aʻu* | *oʻu* | my |
| more than one, any ones | *ni aʻu* | *ni oʻu* | some ... of mine |

Taught in Chapter 25.

## Possessives: the belonging sentence, every owner

| Owner | *a*-word form | *o*-word form |
|---|---|---|
| I | *aʻu* | *oʻu* |
| he, she | *ana* | *ona* |
| we two (you and I) | *a tā* | *o tā* |
| they two | *a lā* | *o lā* |
| we (but not you) | *a mātou* | *o mātou* |

Taught in Chapter 42. The book prints no row for one person addressed ("yours", to one person): that form is not taught in this book.

## Tense-aspect-mood particles

The same sentence run across the four tense-aspect-mood particles this book teaches, from Chapter 4.

| Samoan | English |
|---|---|
| *Sā ʻou alu.* | I went. |
| *Na ʻou alu.* | I went. |
| *ʻUa ʻou alu.* | I have gone. |
| *ʻOleʻā ʻou alu.* | I will go. |

Taught in Chapter 4. This is the four-particle set Chapter 4 teaches; the general particle *e*/*te* and the progressive *ʻoloʻo*, taught later in this book, are not folded into this grid by any chapter.

## Demonstrative

This book teaches one demonstrative, *lenā* ("that"), drilled in Chapter 7 against five naming sentences. Samoan has a fuller set of demonstratives beyond this one; that fuller set is outside this book.

| Samoan | English |
|---|---|
| *ʻO le pusi lenā.* | That is the cat. |
| *ʻO le tama lenā.* | That is the boy. |
| *ʻO le lāʻau lenā.* | That is the tree. |
| *ʻO le ʻāiga lenā.* | That is the family. |
| *ʻO se uō lenā.* | That is a friend. |

Taught in Chapter 7.
